About me

I like problems that fight back.

A tricky graph algorithm at midnight, a computer vision model that refuses to generalize: I'm happiest when I'm digging in until it finally clicks. That habit started with Math Olympiads and science fairs in Bangladesh, and it grew into a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering at Patuakhali Science & Technology University, where I graduated first in my class.

Then I went to the other side of the classroom. As a lecturer at the University of Scholars and later at BUBT, I taught Discrete Mathematics, Numerical Analysis and System Analysis & Design. Teaching showed me something research demands too: you don't really understand an idea until you can explain it clearly to someone else.

Since August 2026 I've been in Oulu, Finland, studying for an M.Sc. in Technology (Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence track) at the University of Oulu. My work centres on deep learning and computer vision, with applications in healthcare, agriculture and intelligent systems. I have 7 publications across journal, conference and book-chapter venues.

What I'm looking for: a research assistant position in a group working on machine learning or computer vision, a teaching assistant role, or a part-time job where I can build useful things with a team. Write to me at shampabanik13@gmail.com or find me on LinkedIn.

Away from the screen, I'm a thriller addict: books by day, movies by night. Maybe that's why I enjoy debugging, since every bug is a mystery with a culprit. I also garden, and I love meeting new people, which turns out to be easier than expected in a country with this much snow.
